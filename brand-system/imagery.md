# Imagery System — The Founder's Foyer

## Subject buckets

We photograph (or commission) three buckets. Anything outside these buckets is off-system.

- **Workspace artifacts** — close-ups of the *tools and objects of the work itself*. Notebooks, terminal screens, whiteboards, half-disassembled devices. Hero / supporting role. The thing that proves the thinking is happening.
- **Threshold spaces** — foyers, hallways, doorways, the edges of rooms. Atmospheric / establishing role. Honors the brand metaphor without being literal.
- **Physical evidence of thinking** — diagrams, contour maps, sketches, dog-eared books, sticky notes. Texture / depth role. Used as background or as crops within other compositions.

We do not photograph people facing the camera. The host and guests appear in audio only.

## Lighting rules

- **Always natural or single-source.** Never flat strobe. Never ring-light.
- **Time of day matters.** Blue hour, late evening, or under workshop fluorescents. **Never noon, never golden-hour glamour.**
- **Hard shadows are fine.** Soft scattered window light is fine. Avoid the soft-glamour aesthetic of lifestyle photography.
- **Single light source preferred.** If multiple, one dominant + one weak fill.

## Material & palette anchors

Every image must contain at least one of these materials:

vellum · copper trace · indigo ink · matte aluminum · dry-erase residue · grid paper · fluorescent tube · oak grain · coated paper · brass · salt-marked leather · graphite

Surfaces lean **cool neutral with one warm or signal point**. Parchment + circuit-green, or parchment + cobalt. Avoid all-warm or all-cool compositions.

## Reference prompts (10)

Format: `subject — lighting — material/palette anchor`

1. **Whiteboard with arrows** — Whiteboard half-filled with arrows and lambda calculus, eraser smudge in the corner — flat fluorescent overhead at 9pm — graphite, dry-erase residue, fingerprint smudge, off-white wall behind.

2. **Kindle + terminal** — Kindle propped against a coffee mug, terminal window glowing on a laptop screen behind — desk lamp from the right, no other light — black plastic, char-marked paper, glass, faint green from the terminal cursor.

3. **Topographic vellum** — Topographic contour map printed on tracing paper, three sheets stacked with a paperclip — north window, blue hour — vellum, indigo ink, brass paperclip, parchment desk surface.

4. **Disassembled pointer** — Broken laser pointer disassembled on a pad of grid paper, components labeled in pencil — workshop bulb overhead — grid lines, copper trace, lithium cell, AA grid paper.

5. **Foyer threshold** — Architectural detail of a foyer — door frame, threshold, stair runner — at 6pm with last light from a window further down the hall — oak wainscot, brass strike plate, dust motes in the cone of light.

6. **Dog-eared book** — Open hardback annotated in pencil, half-empty cup of tea beside it — overhead reading lamp from upper left — foxed paper, ceramic glaze, graphite on margin, salt-marked leather book strap.

7. **Chalk diagram** — Slate or chalkboard with one circle, three arrows, and the word "why?" in the corner, eraser dust at the base — single workshop bulb — chalk, slate, residual dust, faint green chalk smear.

8. **Microphone closeup** — Cardioid mic on a desk arm, foam pop filter visible, a blank notepad open behind — desk lamp from the right, indirect — matte black, foam grey, manila card, the corner of a coffee mug.

9. **Stack of theses** — Three printed essays bound with a black binder clip, top sheet annotated in indigo — late afternoon, west window — copy paper, black plastic clip, indigo ink, faint coffee ring on the corner.

10. **Coffee + grid paper** — Coffee mug on a sheet of A4 grid paper, half-finished diagram visible underneath, pen across the top — overhead lamp — porcelain, A4 grid, blue ballpoint, faint steam.

Each prompt is shootable: a real photographer (or AI tool) can produce the image without inventing missing detail.

## Do not depict

- Smiling people facing the camera (corporate stock photography aesthetic)
- Founder stereotypes — hoodies in front of whiteboards, two people pointing at a laptop
- Isometric illustrations of "workflows" or "ecosystems"
- Gradient meshes as background fill
- "Hustle culture" props — energy drinks, "rise and grind" mugs, motivational posters
- Generative-AI imagery with telltale artifacts (extra fingers, gradient skies, plastic skin)
- Stock business handshakes, happy diverse meeting tables, chairs around a glass conference table
- Anything that looks like it was shot for a SaaS landing page in 2018

## Application notes

- **Show cover (the podcast's master art):** typographic, on `ink` — monogram top-left, title in display type, `signal` accent rule. No photography; the show cover must survive at 55px thumbnail where photographic detail dies. See `applications.html` surface 02 for the canonical composition. Export at 3000×3000.
- **Episode cover art:** cropped detail from a workspace-artifact image, with the wordmark or monogram in the bottom-left corner on a solid `ink` backplate. Square format (1:1), 3000×3000. (Show cover = type; episode covers = photography. Two layers, deliberately distinct.)
- **Social posts:** pull-quote in display type on `ground`, a single workspace-artifact image as a 4:5 background occupying the bottom third.
- **Newsletter header:** small wordmark, no image. Keep it sparse.
- **Website hero:** large workspace-artifact image (full-bleed), with a gradient scrim across the bottom 30% — transparent to `ink` at 60% opacity — holding `ground` headline text. (Not ink-soft: a mid-grey scrim can't guarantee contrast over an unknown photograph; the ink gradient can.)

If a use case isn't covered here, default to the most restrained option. The brand prefers underused space over over-decorated space.
