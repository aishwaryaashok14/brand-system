What if the next 1,000 SaaS products aren’t built by full-stack teams, but by single operators with three AI agents? n=1, but the foyer is full of them. Forty-five minutes. Lightly edited. No conclusions.

voice · sample copy · see tone.md

Raw ideas. Thinking out loud.

This week, Aishwarya and a guest sit with a question worth taking seriously. Not “what should every founder do?” but “what if this thing we all assume is actually wrong?”

You’ll leave with more questions than answers. That’s the point.
