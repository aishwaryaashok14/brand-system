# Voice — The Founder's Foyer

## What we sound like

**Hypothesis-led** — Phrases ideas as questions worth taking seriously. "What if X?" carries more weight here than "X is true." Comfortable not knowing.

**Builder-specific** — Uses concrete product / operations vocabulary. Names the thing (the product, the framework, the bug) instead of metaphor. "Shipped four products in 18 months" beats "scaled rapidly."

**Compressed** — Short sentences. Full stops. Never strung-together adjectives. If a line can be cut, it gets cut.

**First-person plural** — "We sit with the question," not "you'll learn." The host and listener are doing the work together; this isn't a lecture.

**Quiet about its own ambition** — Names what the show is *not* before what it is. The quietness is the brand.

## We are / we are not

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Hypothesis-led | Thought-leadership-confident |
| Builder-specific | Founder-as-mythology |
| Compressed | Long-winded |
| First-person plural | Prescriptive ("you should…") |

## Sample sentences

### Do say

1. "What if the next 1,000 SaaS products are built by single operators with three AI agents?"
2. "This week, Jonny on the four products that taught him to ship before he was ready."
3. "We don't recap. We don't pitch. We sit with the question for forty-five minutes."
4. "n=1, but the foyer is full of them."
5. "Lightly edited. No conclusions."
6. "Aishwarya talks shipping fast, learning faster, and the four products that taught her it was time to leave."
7. "Forty-five minutes with a builder who's still in the middle of the thing."
8. "The mic's on. Pull up a chair."
9. "What's actually keeping you up?"
10. "You'll leave with more questions than answers. That's the point."

### Don't say (and why)

1. "Empower founders to unlock their full potential." — every word is filler; "empower" and "unlock" are banned
2. "In this episode, we'll learn the 5 secrets to building a unicorn." — listicle voice; treats listener as a search engine
3. "Tune in to hear an exclusive interview with…" — 2010 podcast register; "exclusive" is meaningless
4. "Don't miss this game-changing conversation." — "game-changing" is filler; "don't miss" is FOMO marketing
5. "Aishwarya sits down with the legendary Jonny Burger." — hagiography; treats guests as celebrities not peers
6. "We're on a mission to democratize founder knowledge." — "democratize" overpromises; every brand says this
7. "Solutions for the modern entrepreneur." — "solutions" + "modern" = SaaS slop double-feature
8. "Hit subscribe and ring the bell so you never miss a drop!" — YouTube-CTA voice; breaks the editorial register
9. "This is a journey through the world of startups." — "journey" is the most overused metaphor in branding
10. "Cutting-edge insights from world-class founders." — every word in this sentence is in the banned list

## Episode titles

Titles are **hypotheses phrased as questions**, sentence case, no colon-plus-guest-name constructions:

- ✅ "What if SaaS dies?"
- ✅ "Why does every second product fail?"
- ❌ "Jonny Burger: Building Remotion" — directory listing, not a hypothesis
- ❌ "Ep. 42 — The Future of SaaS" — topic label; topics aren't questions

The guest is named in the "with" line, never in the title. The question must be one the episode genuinely doesn't answer — if the episode resolves it, the title overpromised.

## Microcopy snippets

**Primary CTA (hero):**
"Listen to the latest"
*Used at the top of the home page. Replaces "Subscribe now" — we don't ask for the subscription before the listener has heard anything.*

**Empty state (episode index, before publish):**
"Nothing here yet. The next conversation drops {date}."
*Specific date beats "soon." Mirrors the show's commitment to specificity.*

**Error message (audio fails to load):**
"The episode didn't load. Try again, or read the transcript."
*Acknowledges the problem, gives an alternative path. Never "Oops!" or "Something went wrong."*

## Banned words

These are filler. Reject any sentence that uses them:

empower · elevate · seamless · delight · unleash · revolutionize · transform · journey · solutions · world-class · cutting-edge · best-in-class · exclusive · game-changing · democratize · curate (when used as filler) · bespoke · artisanal · pivotal · synergy · leverage (as verb) · unlock · ring the bell · paradigm shift · disruptor · thought leader

## Sentence shapes that work

- **Specific noun + specific number:** "the four products that taught him to ship," "forty-five minutes with a guest"
- **Question that doesn't expect an easy answer:** "What if X?" "What's actually keeping you up?"
- **Sentence ending in a full stop, on its own line.** Like that one.
- **Lowercase aside in brackets, set in mono:** (n=1, but…) — never italic. Manrope has no italic cut; synthesized obliques are off-system. Use the `.aside` class (type.css) or inline mono.
- **First-person plural that includes the listener:** "we sit with," "we don't recap"

## Sentence shapes that don't

- "Are you a founder struggling with X?" — aspirational interrogation, podcast-ad register
- "In this episode you'll discover…" — gameshow setup, breaks the foyer intimacy
- "Tune in every Wednesday for…" — radio-DJ register, doesn't fit
- Three adjectives in a row before a noun ("groundbreaking, inspirational, unmissable")
